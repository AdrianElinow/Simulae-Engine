# Economies

ANEURISM IV uses several overlapping economies instead of one simple currency loop.
You can be rich in Credits and poor in access, or wealthy in access and broke in materials.

## Credit Economy
- [[Credits]] are the ordinary currency.
- They pay for goods, services, property, and most market access.
- They are earned through jobs, sales, and some activities.

## Anamnecyte Economy
- Anamnecytes unlock and upgrade fates.
- Negative Anamnecytes create the punishment chain that can lead to [[Slave]].

## Market Economy
- [[Dealer]] and [[Banker]] interact with DealerNet and Night Market orders.
- [[Night Market]] turns supply into pricing pressure and logistics pressure.
- Scarcity changes what the city will pay for.

## Pressure Economy
- [[Rot]] increases economic friction.
- [[Limitation]] turns law into a cost.
- Events like War and Darkness can temporarily make some goods and labor more valuable.
