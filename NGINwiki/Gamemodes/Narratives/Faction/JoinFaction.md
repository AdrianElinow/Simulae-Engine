# Join Faction

Joining a faction is the entry point for most larger narrative arcs. The player should be able to discover the faction, meet its gatekeepers, satisfy its requirements, and decide whether to join openly, infiltrate, or manipulate it from the inside.

## Join Paths
- Direct recruitment: a recruiter, officer, guild member, or patron offers membership.
- Reputation-based entry: the player gains entry because of prior deeds or public standing.
- Trial-based entry: the faction demands a test, quest, duel, or proving mission.
- Patronage entry: a sponsor, noble, handler, or senior member vouches for the player.
- Covert entry: the player joins under false pretenses or as an undercover asset.

## What The Generator Should Track
- Entry requirements such as money, rank, social standing, skill, secret knowledge, or a required item.
- Faction values such as ideology, legality, secrecy, discipline, greed, piety, or militarism.
- Gatekeeper personalities such as suspicious, practical, ambitious, or idealistic leaders.
- Initial rank, privileges, and restrictions after admission.
- The first task, commission, or mission that confirms the player's place.

## Membership Plot Variants
- Open recruitment: the faction is actively looking for new members.
- Probationary entry: the player is accepted temporarily and must prove themself.
- Trial by task: a mission, exam, duel, or delivery determines acceptance.
- Patron entry: a sponsor, mentor, or handler vouches for the player.
- Debt entry: the player joins because they owe money, favors, or protection.
- Covert entry: the player joins under disguise or as an infiltrator.
- Hereditary entry: bloodline, family, or title gives automatic access.
- Oath entry: membership is sealed by a vow, ritual, or binding promise.

## Player Interactions
- Accept or refuse the offer.
- Ask for better terms, a different assignment, or higher starting rank.
- Join honestly, join reluctantly, or join as an infiltrator.
- Delay membership while gathering leverage or more information.
- Betray the faction after entry, or convert the faction into a new alliance.

## Good Join Scenarios
- A mercenary company offers a rookie contract after a successful rescue.
- A mage guild admits the player after an exam or recovered grimoire.
- A criminal crew brings the player in after a smuggling run.
- A knightly order requires a vow, a relic, or a trial of arms.
- A spy agency recruits the player after a discreet act of competence.
