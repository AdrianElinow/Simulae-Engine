# Items

This folder mirrors the wiki's item taxonomy.
Items are not just inventory slots; they are the player's way of turning the city's systems into leverage.

## Main Categories
- [[Weapons]]
- [[Materials]]
- [[Food & Drink]]
- [[Implements]]
- [[Contraband]]
- [[Drugs & Meds]]
- [[Cosmetics]]

## How Items Fit The Game
- Weapons create force and legal risk.
- Materials feed crafting and maintenance.
- Food and drink keep the body running.
- Implements let the player manipulate the world.
- Contraband changes how Cortex treats you.
- Drugs and meds shift survival, healing, and addiction.
- Cosmetics change appearance without changing the core simulation.
