# Night Market

The Night Market is the city's distributed trade network.
It is where Dealers and Bankers turn supply into money and money into reach.

## How It Works
- Dealers and Bankers can interact with DealerNet and order systems.
- Night Market demand changes with scarcity and Rot pressure.
- Fulfilled orders convert logistics into Credits, goods, and progression.

## World Role
- The Night Market is the economy of logistics.
- It rewards planning, transport, and good information more than raw combat.
- It is one of the clearest places where social economy and material economy overlap.
