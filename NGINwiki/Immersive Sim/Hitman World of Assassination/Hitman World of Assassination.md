# Hitman: World of Assassination

Hitman: World of Assassination is an immersive assassination sandbox about reading routines, bending social systems, and leaving a mission cleaner than you found it.
It is built around four linked layers:
- [[Roles]] define who is in the mission space and how they behave.
- [[Roster]] collects the major factions, recurring characters, and target examples.
- [[Factions]] define who cooperates, who questions, and who escalates.
- [[Systems]] define disguises, suspicion, witnesses, and exit pressure.

## Core Loop
1. Study the venue, the target routine, and the security graph.
2. Move through disguise, social engineering, or environmental manipulation.
3. Isolate the target and create a clean opportunity.
4. Escape before suspicion, witnesses, or lockdown turn the whole map hostile.

## The World
- Agency assets frame the mission and extract the operative.
- Security staff react first, then coordinate wider lockdowns.
- Venue staff keep the place believable and provide cover or clues.
- Civilians become witnesses, distractions, or collateral pressure.
- Targets sit at the center of the mission and force the player to improvise.
