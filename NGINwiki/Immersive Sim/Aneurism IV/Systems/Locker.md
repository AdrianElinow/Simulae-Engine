# Locker

Lockers are persistent storage points that let players keep items between lives.
They are the game's answer to inventory continuity.

## How It Works
- Surface lockers hold normal storage.
- Underground storage can hold Contraband that would be risky elsewhere.
- Lockers let a player preserve gear, materials, and key items across deaths.

## World Role
- Lockers reduce the cost of failure.
- They also make the difference between temporary gains and long-term accumulation.
