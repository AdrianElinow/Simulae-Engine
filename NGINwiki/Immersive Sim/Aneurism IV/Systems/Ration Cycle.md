# Ration Cycle

The Ration Cycle is the city's periodic food-distribution event.
When the system works, it hands out basic survival support to keep the population going.

## How It Works
- A cycle timer triggers a ration event.
- If the relevant machine is functioning and stocked, players can claim basic food support.
- The cycle is one of the simplest ways the city's infrastructure directly feeds the player.

## World Role
- The Ration Cycle keeps starvation from becoming immediate collapse.
- It also creates a predictable point of contention around supply, crowding, and theft.
