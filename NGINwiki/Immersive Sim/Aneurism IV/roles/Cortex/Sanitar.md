# Sanitar

Born to work, forced to fight.

- Alignment: Cortex
- Faction: Cortex
- Roles: Custody, Security
- Upgrade from: [[Liquidator]]
- Costs: 6 anamnecytes
- Alternative: Deputization by a Controller or Limitator during an event
- Permissions: Patrol, Breach Limit Property, Executions, Repair, Corpse Disposal, Rot Burning
- Health: 125
- Loadout: Wrench x 1, Brain Scorcher x 1, Large Repair Kit x 1, CTX: Ammo Pack x 1

The Sanitar merges cleanup and enforcement.
It inherits the city's maintenance duties from the Liquidator while also helping Cortex terminate Limits.

## World Role
- Sanitars are one of the strongest anti-Rot roles in the game.
- They cannot scavenge, so they rely more heavily on purchased supplies.
- They are the Cortex cleaner that can also fight like an officer.
