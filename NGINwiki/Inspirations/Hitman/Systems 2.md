# Systems

Hitman missions live or die on information flow.
This folder captures the systems that turn a venue into a social puzzle.

## Core Systems
- Disguises
- Suspicion
- Witnesses
- Opportunities
- Mission Stories
- Lockdowns
- Exits

## How It Fits Together
- Disguises buy access, but only until the wrong person looks twice.
- Suspicion turns small mistakes into escalating pressure.
- Witnesses convert a private error into a public problem.
- Opportunities and Mission Stories give the player structured improvisation rails.
- Lockdowns and exits determine whether the contract ends cleanly or loudly.
