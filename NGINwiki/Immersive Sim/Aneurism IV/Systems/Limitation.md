# Limitation

Limitation is the city's law state.
When you are Limited, Cortex can treat you as a target for enforcement.

## How It Works
- Illegal items, witnessed crimes, and restricted actions can add Limit.
- Limited players can be executed by a Controller or Limitator.
- Scum are permanently Limited by default.
- A Limitation Order temporarily changes who can enter the Underground and who can be hunted there.

## World Role
- Limitation turns law into a gameplay cost.
- It is the main reason the city has a criminal underworld instead of pure freeform chaos.
