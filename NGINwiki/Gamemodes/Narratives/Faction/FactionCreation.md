# Faction Creation

Faction creation is the origin story for a new institution. The generator should treat it as a combination of ideology, resources, leadership, location, and the first conflict that forces the faction to exist instead of just being a label.

## What A New Faction Needs
- A purpose or ideology: profit, justice, faith, secrecy, conquest, survival, research, or revenge.
- A faction type: mercenary, corporation, criminal organization, military, spy agency, guild, knightly order, mage guild, or a hybrid.
- A founder or founding group: who starts it, why they start it, and what they already control.
- A home base or sphere of influence: a building, district, region, route, institution, or hidden network.
- Initial resources: money, recruits, weapons, spells, contacts, relics, information, or legal standing.
- A first enemy or obstacle: a rival group, an oppressive state, an internal traitor, a debt, or a lack of legitimacy.

## Creation Plot Variants
- Breakaway faction: a splinter group leaves an existing institution to start something new.
- Refugee faction: displaced people build a faction around survival, protection, or revenge.
- Reformed faction: a tarnished organization is rebuilt around new leadership or ideals.
- Startup faction: a small crew turns a niche skill, service, or product into a new power bloc.
- Secret society: the faction begins in hiding and grows through secrecy and trust.
- Rebel cell: the faction forms around opposition to a ruling power or occupying force.
- Restoration order: the faction exists to restore a lost kingdom, relic, doctrine, or tradition.
- Sponsored faction: a patron, noble, state, or corporation quietly bankrolls the new group.

## Faction Creation Beats
- Define the founding problem.
- Assemble the founders and the first loyalists.
- Choose the public face and the hidden truth.
- Claim territory, clients, members, or a mission.
- Create the first questline or signature activity.
- Bind the faction together with rules, rituals, or promises.

## Player Interactions
- Found a faction from scratch.
- Back a faction with money, tools, secrecy, or manpower.
- Merge two partial groups into one faction.
- Rename or rebrand an existing faction.
- Split a faction into a splinter cell or rival organization.

## Good Creation Scenarios
- A mercenary company forms after a war leaves veterans unemployed.
- A mage guild forms around a recovered school of magic and one dangerous grimoire.
- A criminal organization forms when a neighborhood tries to defend itself and becomes a syndicate.
- A knightly order forms around an oath, relic, or broken throne.
- A corporation forms after inventors, lawyers, and investors turn a prototype into an empire.
