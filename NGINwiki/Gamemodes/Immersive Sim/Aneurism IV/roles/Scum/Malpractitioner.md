# Malpractitioner

Do harm.

- Alignment: Scum
- Faction: Scum
- Roles: Anarchy, Salutary, Apothecary, Alchemy
- Upgrade from: [[Corpsman]]
- Costs: 4 anamnecytes
- Permissions: Heal people; Produce Medicine; Refill Autostitchers; Murder; Destruction of Machinery; Creator of Atrocity
- Health: 100
- Loadout: Strange Lighter x 1

The Malpractitioner is the Corpsman turned inside out.
It still understands medicine and healing, but uses that knowledge to support the Scum front line and to craft the worst kinds of outcomes.

## World Role
- Malpractitioners can lace items and weaponize care.
- They can craft Festering Serum at a Pharma Workbench.
- They are the secret bridge from a normal body to the hidden [[Vomit Coffin]] state.
