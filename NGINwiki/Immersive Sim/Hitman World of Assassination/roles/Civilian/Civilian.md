# Civilian

The ambient witness pool.

- Alignment: Civilian
- Faction: Public
- Roles: Observation
- Status: Ambient
- Tools: Phones, gossip, panic

## Role Summary
Civilian behavior keeps the space alive, noisy, and vulnerable to panic. They are not the target, but they can turn a quiet mistake into a public mess.

## Example Fixtures
- Use for witnesses, crowd movement, panic spread, and noncombatant behavior.
- Good fixture for public-space logic because civilians make the venue feel occupied instead of scripted.
