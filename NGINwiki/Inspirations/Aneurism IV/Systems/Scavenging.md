# Scavenging

Scavenging is the practice of looting public trash, dumpsters, and hidden caches for useful items.
It is one of the core survival loops in ANEURISM IV.

## How It Works
- Proles rely on it heavily.
- Cortex fates do not scavenge.
- Underground scavenging tends to be more dangerous and more contraband-heavy than surface scavenging.

## World Role
- Scavenging turns city waste into player agency.
- It is one of the clearest examples of the game's "everything is a system" design.
