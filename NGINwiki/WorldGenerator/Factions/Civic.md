# Civic 

## Interests
- Community welfare and interests
- representation
- participation
- education
- advocacy
- volunteerism
- social cohesion
- transparency

## Tendencies
- coalition building
- decentralization
- advocacy expansion
- grant dependence

## Failure States
- Inefficiency
- Activism without action
- donor capture (donors become leadership)
- moral absolutism
- astro-turfing
- mission drift
- political capture
- fraud and abuse

## Conflict

### vs Politic

- Sovereignty
	- Community Autonomy vs State control
- Conflict of Interest
	- Public advocacy <-> government policy
	- Civil rights <-> security measures
	- Grassroots reform <-> institutional preservation
- Conflict of Purpose
	- Protest <-> crowd control
	- Public accountability <-> bureaucratic resistance
	- Civic mobilization <-> administrative containment
