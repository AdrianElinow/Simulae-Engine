# Food & Drink

Food and drink are consumable survival items.
They keep the body from failing and are part of the game's basic nourishment loop.

## How They Work
- Food and drink restore or maintain survival needs such as Nourishment and Moisture.
- They may also interact with blood, stamina, mood, or addiction depending on the item.
- Some food is ration-grade; some is luxury; some is borderline contraband.

## World Role
- Food and drink tie the Ration Cycle to the rest of the economy.
- They make scarcity visible in a way that every player can understand.
