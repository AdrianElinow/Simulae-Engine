# Materials

Materials are the raw building blocks used for crafting, repair, and trade.
They are the backbone of the city's supply economy.

## How They Work
- Materials are usually found through Scavenging, dismantling, or specialized production.
- They can be refined into better versions or combined into finished goods.
- Their value changes with scarcity, Rot, and event pressure.

## Common Uses
- Crafting weapons and tools
- Repairing machinery and structures
- Producing consumables and medical supplies
- Feeding Night Market orders and logistics chains

## World Role
- Materials are what turn trash into civilization.
- They are the most obvious example of the game converting city waste into player power.
