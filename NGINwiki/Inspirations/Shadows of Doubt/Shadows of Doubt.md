# Shadows of Doubt

Shadows of Doubt is a procedural detective immersive sim about a living city, citizen routines, and cases solved through evidence instead of script.
It is the clearest benchmark for systemic investigation at city scale.

## Core Loop
1. Enter a procedurally generated city and take a case.
2. Follow routines, addresses, records, and witness trails to build a theory.
3. Test the theory by gathering evidence and narrowing suspects.
4. Resolve the case, even if the city keeps generating new problems afterward.

## Required Systems
- Procedural generation for cities, citizens, and cases.
- Stable citizen identities, routines, schedules, addresses, and relationships.
- Evidence collection, deduction, and case-book style tracking.
- Tools for breaking into spaces, searching records, and cross-referencing clues.
- Consequence handling for wrong accusations, failed leads, and dead-end investigations.

## Factions
- City Hall: the civic processing layer and case submission sink.
- Starch Kola Enforcers: the privatized police and authority structure.
- Citizens and apartment communities: the everyday social fabric.
- Businesses and landlords: the access, rent, and workplace layer.
- Criminals and suspects: the hidden threat layer under the city.

## Signature NPC Roles
- Private investigator: the player role and investigation loop.
- Suspect, victim, and witness: the core case graph.
- Landlord, tenant, shopkeeper, clerk, and office worker: the routine-driven city population.
- Enforcer, guard, and security worker: the response layer when the law gets involved.
- City Hall clerk and records staff: the bureaucratic interface that turns evidence into outcomes.

## Signature Tools and Evidence
- Fingerprints: the core forensic identity tool.
- Surveillance camera footage: the visual proof layer.
- Address books, apartment logs, and citizen records: the routine and identity graph.
- Lockpicks and door bypass tools: the access layer.
- Bribes, cash, and rent money: the economy behind getting information and staying housed.
- Case submission forms and arrest paperwork: the formal resolution layer.
- Apartment management and personal storage: the persistence layer that makes the detective life feel lived in.

## Case and City Systems
- Procedurally generated citizens with routines, jobs, and social links.
- Randomized murder methods and false leads.
- Time-sensitive evidence and investigation deadlines.
- Wrong accusations that punish the player and leave the real killer active.
- Social credit progression that turns solved cases into long-term advancement.

## Narrative Shape
- Emergent detective stories that come from simulation rather than fixed mission scripts.
- A city that feels lived in because every person has a routine and a role.
- Mystery resolution driven by the player's notes and evidence graph, not only by markers.

## Simulae Notes
- Best reference for procedural social simulation and evidence-based investigation.
- Good benchmark for making generated citizens feel legible enough to investigate.
- Strong model for city routines, evidence chains, and case-processing systems that can support future stories.
