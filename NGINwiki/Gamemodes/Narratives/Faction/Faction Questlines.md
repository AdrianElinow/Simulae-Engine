# Faction Questlines

Faction questlines are the longer arcs that teach the player how a faction behaves, what it values, and how far they can rise inside it. They should feel different per faction type, but all share a ladder of trust, rank, and responsibility.

## Questline Structure
- Introduction: the faction tests the player or gives a low-risk job.
- Apprenticeship: the player learns the faction's methods and vocabulary.
- Advancement: the player gains rank, access, and responsibility.
- Specialization: the player branches into a role such as fixer, captain, archmage, investigator, or lieutenant.
- Elite Work: the player receives high-stakes missions that affect faction strategy.
- Leadership Path: the player can become the face, boss, master, or leader of the faction.

## Questline Content Types
- Repeatable work: patrols, deliveries, escorts, audits, investigations, repairs, or raids.
- Reputation gates: the faction withholds better quests until trust is earned.
- Branch missions: moral, political, or strategic choices change future opportunities.
- Rival missions: another member or subfaction competes for the same promotion or prize.
- Consequence missions: failed work should create debt, suspicion, demotion, or war.

## Rank And Reward Ideas
- Access to faction stores, shelters, safehouses, spells, gear, or routes.
- Uniforms, insignia, titles, legal authority, and public recognition.
- Special commands such as calling backup, requesting transport, or opening restricted areas.
- Personal perks such as wages, training, housing, research, or political protection.

## Questline Plot Variants
- Errand chain: the faction starts with small delivery or retrieval jobs.
- Patrol chain: the player repeatedly secures roads, borders, routes, or districts.
- Investigation chain: the faction sends the player to uncover fraud, betrayal, or threats.
- Infiltration chain: the player enters hostile spaces to gather leverage or sabotage.
- Rescue chain: the player extracts allies, assets, or captives from danger.
- Procurement chain: the faction wants tools, relics, supplies, or specialist equipment.
- Strike team chain: the player becomes the responder for high-risk faction operations.
- Diplomatic chain: the player brokers alliances, treaties, or ceasefires.
- Cleanup chain: the faction uses the player to erase mistakes, witnesses, or scandals.
- Leadership trial: the final arc tests whether the player can command the faction.

## Questline Examples
- Mercenary company: contract chain, veteran promotion, elite squad leadership.
- Corporation: onboarding, department promotion, internal audit, board-level crisis.
- Criminal organization: small-time jobs, capo work, turf war, boss succession.
- Military: enlistment, field promotions, campaign commands, theater leadership.
- Spy agency: handler work, covert assignments, blackmail chains, station chief role.
- Guild: apprenticeship, mastery, commission reputation, master seat.
- Knightly order: page or initiate, knighted service, relic quest, grandmaster politics.
- Mage guild: student, adept, researcher, archmage contender.
