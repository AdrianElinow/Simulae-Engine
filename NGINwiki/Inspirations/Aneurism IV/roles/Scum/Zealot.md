# Zealot

The darker waters call for you.

- Alignment: Scum
- Faction: Scum
- Roles: Anarchy, Idolatry
- Upgrade from: [[Scumbag]]
- Costs: 10 anamnecytes
- Alternative: Consecration by another Zealot after they consumed Suspicious Meat
- Permissions: Murder; Destruction of Machinery; Consecration of new members
- Health: 225
- Loadout: Strange Lighter x 1, Ritual Axe x 1

The Zealot is the cult fate.
It is the Scum that can interact with Ritual Pits, consecrate others, and turn the Rot into a religion instead of just a strategy.

## World Role
- Zealots are the only Scum fate that can work with Ritual Pits.
- They drive the Darkness event by feeding bodies into the pit.
- They are the religious end of the Scum path.
