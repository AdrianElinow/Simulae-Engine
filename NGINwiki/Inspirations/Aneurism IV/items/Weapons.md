# Weapons

Weapons are the direct-force items in ANEURISM IV.
They solve problems quickly, but they also draw attention, create Limitation, and reshape the law state of a district.

## What Counts As A Weapon
- Cortex weapons that are legal for enforcement fates.
- Craftable weapons made from scavenged materials.
- Improvised weapons used by desperate or resourceful players.
- Nonlethal and lethal variants both count if they can force a fight.

## How They Fit The Game
- Weapons are the fastest way to create heat.
- Weapons are often Contraband for non-Cortex fates.
- They become much more powerful when paired with an exit route, storage, or a support fate.
