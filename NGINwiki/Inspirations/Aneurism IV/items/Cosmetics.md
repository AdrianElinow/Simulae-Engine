# Cosmetics

Cosmetics are visual items that change how a fate looks without changing the core simulation.

## How They Work
- Cosmetics are aesthetic rather than systemic.
- They can customize a fate's appearance without affecting Rot, Limitation, or combat power.
- They sit beside the survival and crafting categories instead of inside them.

## World Role
- Cosmetics give players a way to express identity inside a harsh system.
- They are the least mechanically important item type, but they still matter socially.
