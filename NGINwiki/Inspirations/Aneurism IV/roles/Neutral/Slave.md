# Slave

The punished.

- Alignment: Neutral
- Faction: Cortex
- Roles: Slavery
- Upgrade to: [[Vomit Coffin]]
- Costs: Anamnecyte Debt
- Permissions: Obey
- Health: 100

This is a classified punishment state.
If you die with negative Anamnecytes, you skip Spirit and become a Slave instead, trapped in the Volos Precinct labor camp until you work off your debt.

## World Role
- Slaves exist as the city's debt recovery state.
- They are the consequence of failing to keep your Anamnecyte balance positive.
- Their path leads to the hidden [[Vomit Coffin]] transformation.
