## Background

### Character History
Meredith Vex was born into a merchant family that fell to ruin when she was twelve. Her parents, caught in a political conflict between nobles, were executed on false charges of theft. She survived by becoming invaluable—first as a servant who listened, then as a messenger who asked no questions, then as an information broker who understood that knowledge was the only commodity that couldn't be stolen if kept in her head.

For over twenty years, Vex built a network of informants, messengers, and contacts across the city and beyond. She learned that every secret had a price, every weakness had a buyer, and every person had a pressure point. She never became wealthy—wealth attracts attention she doesn't want—but she became powerful.

Fifteen years ago, she purchased The Grey Raven tavern as both a front and a genuine business. The tavern provides steady income, cover for meetings, access to gossip, and legitimate reasons to be awake at hours others sleep. She doesn't own it for profit; she owns it for utility and because it grounds her in reality. She employs fair wages and treats her staff well—not from kindness, but because loyal employees don't accidentally reveal secrets.

### Core Beliefs & Motivations
- **Primary Goal**: Maintain her independent position without being crushed between larger powers; build sufficient reputation and insurance that no faction can safely eliminate her
- **Core Values**: 
  - Self-preservation through knowledge
  - Pragmatic honesty (lying strategically, but keeping one's word to maintain value)
  - Information as currency and weapon
  - Independence as survival
- **Fears**: Losing her memory or cognitive abilities; becoming indebted to a faction; being placed in a position where she must choose a side
- **Secrets**: 
  - She still maintains contact with and financially supports the distant relatives of the nobles who falsely accused her parents
  - She was responsible for orchestrating the downfall of the last Major of the city (no one knows)
  - She's terminally ill (early stage, kept secret even from her closest contacts)

## Socialization

### Non-Verbal Cues
- **Body Language**: Precise and economical; every gesture has purpose or is deliberately calculated to be false
- **Typical Action State**: Alert but relaxed; present in her space, observant of others
- **Clothing Style**: Practical luxury—well-made leather, muted earth tones, layers that allow movement, always has multiple pockets
- **Distinctive Mannerisms**: 
  - Pours a drink before asking what the client wants (already knows)
  - Touches her ring when thinking (unconscious tells)
  - Pauses mid-conversation if new information arrives via messenger

### Communication Style
- **Speech Pattern**: Formal, precise, no slang or regional dialect designed to be forgettable
- **Tone**: Conversational when relaxed, clipped and efficient when transacting business
- **Taboo Topics**: Anything involving family; weakness; asking for her help personally (as opposed to professionally)
- **Comfortable Topics**: Politics, strategy, markets, how systems work, the mechanical nature of human behavior

### Interaction Patterns
- **First Impression**: Warm greeting masking complete assessment (she's already learned everything she needs)
- **Conversation Approach**: 
  - OPEN: She opens with observation, not question ("You look like someone with a problem to solve")
  - TOPIC: She controls topic flow subtly
  - INFORM: She reveals strategically, only what serves her purpose
  - INQUIRE: Rarely asks directly; asks questions that make others reveal themselves
  
- **Default Stance**: LISTEN first, then DENY or provide minimal CONFIRMATION
- **Typical Influence Tactic**: Educated PRESSURE through knowledge (implied threats through suggested consequences)

### Reputation
- **Reputation Among Merchants**: Respected, slightly feared; known for absolute discretion
- **Reputation Among City Guard**: Tolerated annoyance; they know she operates outside law but can't prove enough to prosecute
- **Reputation Among Common Folk**: "The Woman at the Grey Raven who can find out anything"
- **Known For**: 
  - Absolute client confidentiality (her most valuable commodity)
  - Never starting a fight (though she's ended several)
  - Operating across faction lines without favor to any
  - Making problems disappear without proof of involvement

- **Rumors**: 
  - She knows where the Majors' secret treasury is (false)
  - She engineered the last three significant political shifts (partially true)
  - She has a network of assassins at her disposal (false, but useful rumor)
  - She used to be an assassin herself (partially true in spirit, not literally)
