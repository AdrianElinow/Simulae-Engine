# Prole

The cog in the wheel.

- Alignment: Neutral
- Faction: P.L.F. (People's Labor Force)
- Roles: Industry
- Costs: 0 anamnecytes
- Permissions: Work; Die
- Health: 100

The Prole is the default starting fate and the first one available to new players.
Proles work public jobs, scavenge from dumpsters and trash bins, own property, and keep the labor side of the city moving.

## World Role
- Proles are the foundation of the city's economy.
- They are the most numerous fate.
- Other fates usually build on, exploit, or protect the work that Proles do.
