# Self-defense

Self-defense is the law exception that lets a player retaliate without becoming the aggressor.

## How It Works
- If a Neutral player attacks, pushes, or steals from you, the game can treat your response as justified.
- Self-defense keeps the city from punishing every retaliation equally.
- It is one of the few ways ordinary players can answer violence without instantly becoming the criminal.

## World Role
- Self-defense makes the city's law system feel legible instead of arbitrary.
- It is one of the reasons Neutral play can survive inside a hostile city.
