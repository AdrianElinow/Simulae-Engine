# Group / Faction Plots

## Character And Identity Plots
- Origin story: a character's background, formative trauma, education, or first calling.
- Apprenticeship: training under a mentor, faction, guild, or school before independence.
- Rivalry: a recurring contest for rank, attention, recognition, or resources.
- Secret identity: a hidden role, double life, disguise, alias, or undercover cover.
- Exile and return: the character is driven away, changes elsewhere, then comes back changed.
- Hidden lineage: parentage, inheritance, bloodline, or lost family ties are revealed.
- Fall from grace: a respected figure loses status, credibility, or moral standing.
- Redemption: the character tries to repair past damage or reclaim lost honor.
- Retirement and succession: the old role is handed off, stolen, or inherited.
- Missing person: someone vanishes, is presumed dead, or is otherwise out of contact.

## Investigation And Mystery Plots
- Murder case: the player follows the clues behind a death.
- Missing person: the player searches for a vanished character or group.
- Locked-room mystery: a crime seems impossible until the hidden route is found.
- Cold case: an old unsolved event is reopened by new evidence.
- Serial crime: repeated incidents point to a pattern or offender.
- Fraud exposure: theft, embezzlement, or manipulation is uncovered through evidence.
- Cult or conspiracy: the investigation reveals a larger hidden organization.
- False suspect: the obvious culprit is wrong and the truth is elsewhere.
- Alibi collapse: a supposed timeline is broken by evidence or witness testimony.
- Evidence trail: the story is driven by clues, records, and cross-checking.

## Conflict And War Plots
- Border raid: a sudden attack tests defenses, response time, and local control.
- Siege: a location is surrounded, starved, bombarded, or cut off.
- Occupation: a force takes control and imposes new rules.
- Insurgency: a resistance cell fights back from within or behind enemy lines.
- Convoy defense: people, supplies, or relics must be escorted safely.
- Prisoner rescue: captured allies or civilians must be recovered.
- Mutiny: rank-and-file members reject command or rebel against leadership.
- Desertion: soldiers or retainers abandon the cause.
- Liberation: a territory, city, or faction is reclaimed from an occupier.
- Ceasefire enforcement: peace only holds if both sides can be made to obey it.

## Exploration And Discovery Plots
- Ruin delve: the player explores a forgotten structure or dungeon.
- Lost city: a vanished settlement, base, or civilization is rediscovered.
- Hidden wing: secret rooms, tunnels, archives, or wings are uncovered.
- Map recovery: the player finds, steals, or reconstructs route knowledge.
- Artifact hunt: a meaningful object must be tracked down through clues.
- Expedition rescue: an exploration team goes missing and must be retrieved.
- First contact: a new culture, species, or hidden group is encountered.
- Hidden route: a shortcut, passage, or escape line changes future movement.
- Ancient machine activation: old technology is repaired, powered, or awakened.
- Treasure recovery: wealth, relics, or legacy items are pulled from danger.

## Economy And Logistics Plots
- Trade war: rival groups use pricing, routes, or tariffs as weapons.
- Embargo: access to goods or territory is blocked.
- Supply shortage: scarcity forces rationing, substitution, or desperate errands.
- Caravan escort: goods, pilgrims, or cargo must travel through danger.
- Labor strike: workers stop cooperating until terms change.
- Debt collection: payment, collateral, or enforcement becomes the plot engine.
- Market manipulation: a group tries to corner, crash, or distort the market.
- Monopoly defense: a dominant actor tries to keep exclusive control.
- Resource extraction: control over mines, wells, farms, or fuel becomes contested.
- Customs audit: shipments, permits, and records are inspected for smuggling or fraud.

## Supernatural And Occult Plots
- Curse: a bad state, misfortune, or magical burden follows the subject.
- Haunting: a place or person is troubled by spirits or lingering presence.
- Exorcism: a possession or curse is forcibly removed.
- Ritual interruption: a magical or religious working is stopped mid-course.
- Demon pact: power is gained through a dangerous bargain.
- Prophecy: a predicted future becomes a driver for action or sabotage.
- Magical duel: rivals settle their conflict through spells or arcane combat.
- Forbidden spell: dangerous knowledge tempts the player or an NPC.
- Possession: an outside force takes control of a body or mind.
- Soul rescue: the story centers on recovering, freeing, or protecting a soul.

## Disaster And Survival Plots
- Plague: sickness spreads and changes movement, trust, and public safety.
- Famine: food scarcity reshapes behavior, politics, and morality.
- Quarantine: borders or districts are sealed to contain a threat.
- Fire, flood, storm, or collapse: a physical disaster changes the map.
- Evacuation: the goal is to move people or valuables out safely.
- Search and rescue: trapped or lost characters must be found quickly.
- Bunker defense: a shelter or outpost must survive sustained pressure.
- Contamination cleanup: toxins, infection, or corruption must be contained.
