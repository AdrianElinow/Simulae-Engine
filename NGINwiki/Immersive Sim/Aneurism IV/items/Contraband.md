# Contraband

Contraband is anything the city does not want Cortex to see.
If it is found on you, it can become a law problem.

## How It Works
- Contraband includes illegal weapons, restricted drugs, illicit tools, and other banned goods.
- Being searched or caught on camera with Contraband can create Limitation.
- Some items are legal in one context and Contraband in another.

## World Role
- Contraband creates the smuggling layer of the game.
- It makes stealth, storage, and route planning matter even when no one is shooting.
