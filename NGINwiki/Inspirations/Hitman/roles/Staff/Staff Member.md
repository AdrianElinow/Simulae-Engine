# Staff Member

The face of normality inside the venue.

- Alignment: Staff
- Faction: Venue Staff
- Roles: Service
- Status: Active
- Tools: Trays, keycards, routine routes

## Role Summary
Staff members keep the place believable, notice odd behavior, and sometimes open the path forward by accident.

## Example Fixtures
- Use for routine coverage, access routes, clue delivery, and accidental openings.
- Good for testing the social camouflage layer that makes disguise play feel earned.
