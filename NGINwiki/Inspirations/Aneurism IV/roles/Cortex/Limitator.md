# Limitator

The executioner.

- Alignment: Cortex
- Faction: Cortex
- Roles: Security, Sodality
- Upgrade from: [[Controller]]
- Costs: 17 anamnecytes
- Permissions: Patrol; Breach Limit Property; Execution
- Health: 425
- Loadout: Flashlight x 1, CTX: SURMA x 1, Nightstick x 1, CTX: Ammo Pack x 1

The Limitator is the heavy Cortex soldier.
It does the Controller's job with more authority, more survivability, and more direct force.

## World Role
- Limitators are the most powerful direct Cortex enforcement fate.
- They can deputize Liquidators into Sanitars during suitable events.
- They represent the city's willingness to solve problems with overwhelming force.
