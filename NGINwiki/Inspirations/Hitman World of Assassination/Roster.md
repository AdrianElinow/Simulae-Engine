# Series Roster

This is the shared example bank for Hitman: World of Assassination.
Use it for future fixtures, target ledgers, and relationship tests.

## How To Use This Page
- Factions name the institutions that shape trust and escalation.
- Archetypes name the reusable NPC shapes.
- Named characters and targets supply concrete fixtures for tests.
- Fixture packs show ready-made combinations for scenario coverage.

## Factions and Institutions
- ICA / Agency: contract operators, handlers, and extraction support.
- Providence: the global power structure behind the trilogy.
- The Partners: Carl Ingram, Marcus Stuyvesant, and Alexa Carlisle.
- The Constant / Arthur Edwards: Providence's broker and control point.
- Lucas Grey's militia: the rebel network that hunts Providence.
- IAGO: the Paris spy and blackmail ring.
- Ether / Ether Biotech: the pharma and data-backed corporate layer.
- Delgado cartel: the Santa Fortuna drug empire.
- The Maelstrom's underworld network: Mumbai's criminal and smuggling web.
- The Ark Society: Janus' survivalist elite organization.
- Haven: the identity-reassignment corporation in the Maldives.
- Kronstadt / Knox Industries: Miami's racing and manufacturing power base.
- Sanguine: Paris fashion as social camouflage.
- Yakuza / Japanese organized crime: the pressure behind Yuki Yamazaki.
- Liberation: the Patient Zero cult and virus network.
- Ambrose Island factions: militia, pirates, and local operators.
- ICA field teams: the in-house agency side used for Berlin and internal security stories.

## Core NPC Archetypes
- Agent / assassin: 47, Grey, and the other field operatives.
- Handler / support: Diana Burnwood and Olivia Hall.
- Security chief / close protection: guards, bodyguards, and response leads.
- Staff / service worker: chefs, servers, attendants, drivers, clerks, and technicians.
- Civilian / guest: tourists, reporters, bystanders, family members, and crowds.
- Executive / board member: corporate leaders, tycoons, financiers, and lawyers.
- Scientist / engineer: lab directors, surgeons, analysts, and system operators.
- Politician / diplomat: ministers, ambassadors, officials, and campaign figures.
- Criminal boss / lieutenant: cartel heads, smugglers, and militia commanders.
- Cult / society leader: secret-society patrons, cult figures, and ritual enforcers.
- Entertainer / celebrity: actors, singers, models, athletes, and media personalities.
