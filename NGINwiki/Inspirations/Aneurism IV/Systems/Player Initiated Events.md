# Player Initiated Events

Player initiated events are deliberate actions that become meaningful enough for the world to react.
They start as a choice and end as a state change.

## Common Examples
- Starting an [[Activities|Activity]] or job.
- Filling a [[Night Market]] order.
- Scavenging a neighborhood dry.
- Stealing, murdering, repairing, or sabotaging.
- Burning [[Rot]], cleansing corpses, or performing ritual actions.

## Why They Matter
- These events produce Credits, Anamnecytes, or both.
- They generate heat, Limitation, trust, or reputation depending on who sees them.
- They often seed a larger [[World Events|world event]] if the district is already under pressure.
- They are how a player's small action becomes part of the city's memory.
