# Vomit Coffin

The foulest transformation state in the game.

- Status: Hidden spoiler fate
- Origin: Usually created by using Festering Serum on a [[Slave]]

The Vomit Coffin is a classified end state rather than a normal public role.
It represents the final, most cursed outcome of the punishment and corruption chain.

## World Role
- It is intentionally obscured by the wiki.
- It exists as the worst thing that can happen to the city's people after debt, rot, and malpractice collide.
- The game treats it as a spoiler state for a reason.
