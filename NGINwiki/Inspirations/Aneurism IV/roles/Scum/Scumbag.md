# Scumbag

Nothing but a rebel.

- Alignment: Scum
- Faction: Scum
- Roles: Anarchy
- Upgrades to: [[Zealot]]
- Costs: 5 anamnecytes
- Permissions: Murder; Destruction of Machinery
- Health: 125
- Loadout: Strange Lighter x 1, Switchblade x 1

The Scumbag is the basic Scum fate.
It exists to destroy Cortex infrastructure, fight the city, and spread disorder wherever it can.

## World Role
- Scumbags are the cheapest Scum entry point.
- Their actions contribute directly to Rot and instability.
- They are permanently limited by the law of the city.
