# Activities

Activities are the jobs and work loops that turn time into Credits or Anamnecytes.
They are the direct, player-facing side of the economy.

## How They Work
- Different fates unlock different activities.
- Proles get the broadest access to common labor.
- Specialized fates get specialized work such as medicine, cleanup, patrol, market handling, or ritual activity.

## Examples
- Ration work
- Factory work
- Repair work
- Cleanup work
- Market orders
- Patrols

## World Role
- Activities keep the city from feeling static.
- They are the main way the player turns a fate into a living role inside the world.
