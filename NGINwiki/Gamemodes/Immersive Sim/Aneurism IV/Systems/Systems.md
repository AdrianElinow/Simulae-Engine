## How It Fits Together
- Credits move the ordinary economy.
- Anamnecytes move fate progression.
- Rot and Limitation move pressure and law.
- Activities, Scavenging, and Night Market orders move the player through the city.
- World events change the map for everyone.
- Player initiated events are the moments when a player's choice becomes a citywide consequence.
