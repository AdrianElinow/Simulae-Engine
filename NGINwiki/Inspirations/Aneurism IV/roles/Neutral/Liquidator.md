# Liquidator

The cleaner of ANEURISM IV.

- Alignment: Neutral
- Faction: P.L.F. (People's Labor Force)
- Roles: Custody
- Upgrades to: [[Sanitar]]
- Costs: 6 anamnecytes
- Permissions: Repair; Corpse Disposal; Rot Burner
- Health: 100
- Loadout: Wrench x 1, Cremator x 1, Small Repair Kit x 2

The Liquidator keeps the city from rotting.
It burns Rot growths, disposes of bodies, refuels generators, and repairs broken machinery.

## World Role
- Liquidators are the maintenance line between a functioning city and a collapsing one.
- They can scavenge public trash and turn that into repair capacity.
- They are the neutral route into the Cortex-aligned [[Sanitar]].
