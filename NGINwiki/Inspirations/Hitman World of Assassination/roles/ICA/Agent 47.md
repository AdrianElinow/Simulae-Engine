# Agent 47

The silent professional in the middle of the social maze.

- Alignment: Agency
- Faction: ICA
- Roles: Infiltration
- Status: Active
- Tools: Coins, silenced pistol, fiber wire

## Role Summary
Agent 47 is the player fantasy for the mode. He studies routines, borrows disguises, creates clean opportunities, and leaves before the venue realizes what happened.

## Example Fixtures
- Use for stealth routing, disguise chains, and target isolation.
- Pair with Diana Burnwood, guards, and civilians to test access, witness, and escalation logic.
