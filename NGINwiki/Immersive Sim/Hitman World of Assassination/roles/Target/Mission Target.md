# Mission Target

The contract's center of gravity.

- Alignment: Target
- Faction: Contract Principal
- Roles: Objective
- Status: Primary
- Tools: Routine, guards, escape routes

## Role Summary
The Mission Target is the person the player is trying to isolate. Their habits, protection detail, and routines are the puzzle the rest of the mission is built around.

## Example Fixtures
- Use for objective selection, route planning, protection detail, and escape-window tests.
- Good anchor for mission structure because every other role exists to support or obstruct the target.
