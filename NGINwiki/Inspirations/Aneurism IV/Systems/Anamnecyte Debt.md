# Anamnecyte Debt

Anamnecyte Debt is what happens when your fate currency goes below zero.
It is the punishment side of the progression economy.

## How It Works
- If you die while in debt, you can fall into the [[Slave]] fate.
- Debt is the game's way of tying failure directly to your future role.
- It makes money, death, and character growth part of the same system.

## World Role
- Anamnecyte Debt is the bridge between normal play and the hidden punishment chain.
- It gives the game a hard consequence without ending the run outright.
