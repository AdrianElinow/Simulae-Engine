# Dealer

You want it, I got it.

- Alignment: Neutral
- Faction: M.B. (Midnight Bloc)
- Roles: Merchandry
- Upgrades to: [[Banker]]
- Costs: 3 anamnecytes
- Permissions: DealerNet
- Health: 100

The Dealer is the city's merchant fate.
It runs Night Market orders, buys and sells goods through DealerNet, and keeps the supply side of the city moving.

## World Role
- Dealers are one of the two fates that can work Night Market orders.
- They are the bridge between player demand and the city's distributed supply.
- A Prole can upgrade into a Dealer while keeping earlier items and Credits.
