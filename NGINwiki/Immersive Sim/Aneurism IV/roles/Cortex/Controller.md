# Controller

Destructor of dissent and rebellion. Enemy of the rot.

- Alignment: Cortex
- Faction: Cortex
- Roles: Security
- Upgrades to: [[Limitator]] or [[Rogue]]
- Costs: 8 anamnecytes
- Permissions: Patrol; Breach Limit Property; Execution
- Health: 150
- Loadout: Flashlight x 1, CTX: Ahti x 1, Nightstick x 1, CTX: Ammo Pack x 1, Bandage x 2

The Controller is the Cortex cop.
It inspects patrols, confiscates Contraband, executes Limits, and keeps the city's law intact.

## World Role
- Controllers are the main face of Cortex authority.
- They can commend citizens for paycycle bonuses.
- They are also the gatekeepers to both the heavy enforcement path and the traitor path.
