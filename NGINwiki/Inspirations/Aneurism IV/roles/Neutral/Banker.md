# Banker

Everything around me is nothing more than filth and clients.

- Alignment: Neutral
- Faction: M.B. (Midnight Bloc)
- Roles: Merchandry, Pecuniary
- Upgrade from: [[Dealer]]
- Costs: 3 anamnecytes
- Permissions: DealerNet
- Health: 100

The Banker is the fate that handles money.
It can store Credits between lives, fulfill DealerNet orders, and operate the same market infrastructure as the [[Dealer]].

## World Role
- Bankers keep money from evaporating on death.
- They are the safest long-term home for player wealth.
- The Banker is the corporate finish of the Dealer path.
