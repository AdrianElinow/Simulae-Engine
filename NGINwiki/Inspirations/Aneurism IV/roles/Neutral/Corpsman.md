# Corpsman

The one who does no harm.

- Alignment: Neutral
- Faction: P.L.F. (People's Labor Force)
- Roles: Salutary, Apothecary
- Upgrades to: [[Malpractitioner]]
- Costs: 3 anamnecytes
- Permissions: Produce Medicine, Refill Autostitchers, Restock Pharmadistributors, Heal people
- Health: 100

The Corpsman is the city's reliable healer.
It can heal other players by spending blood, and it naturally regenerates blood over time, making it far more durable than most Neutral fates.

## World Role
- Corpsmen keep workers active and functional.
- They are one of the main sources of medicine production and medical maintenance.
- Their upgrade path twists cleanly into the Scum-aligned [[Malpractitioner]].
