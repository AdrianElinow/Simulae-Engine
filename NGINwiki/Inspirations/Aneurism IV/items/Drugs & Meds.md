# Drugs & Meds

Drugs and meds are consumables that change the body.
They can heal, buff, sedate, or destabilize the player.

## How They Work
- Meds restore health or clear bad conditions.
- Drugs can provide power, stamina, or other temporary advantages.
- Repeated use can create dependence or side effects.
- Some medical items are lawful; some are not; some become worse when laced.

## World Role
- Drugs and meds sit between survival and risk.
- They are one of the clearest places where the game ties medicine to social control.
