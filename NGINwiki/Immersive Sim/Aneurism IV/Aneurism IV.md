# ANEURISM IV

ANEURISM IV is an immersive multiplayer industrial nightmare simulator set in a rotting city-state.
The game is built around three linked layers:
- [[Fates]] define who the player is while possessed.
- [[Systems]] define how the city, law, rot, money, and events behave.
- [[Items]] define what can be carried, crafted, traded, or used to bend those systems.

## Core Loop
1. Spawn as a [[Spirit]] and choose a fate.
2. Work, scavenge, trade, or enforce order to earn Anamnecytes and Credits.
3. Push the world toward your faction's goals or survive the pressure of the opposing side.
4. React to Rot, Limitation, Night Market demand, and server-wide events before they react to you.

## The World
- Neutral fates keep the city running, making money, cleaning corpses, healing workers, and moving goods.
- Cortex fates enforce order, confiscate contraband, and execute limits.
- Scum fates spread Rot, destroy machinery, and weaponize the Underground.
- Hidden spoiler fates exist for the punishment chain and its worst outcomes.
