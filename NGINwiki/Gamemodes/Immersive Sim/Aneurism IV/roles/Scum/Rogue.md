# Rogue

A traitorous propagator of the rot.

- Alignment: Scum
- Faction: Scum
- Roles: Anarchy
- Upgrade from: [[Controller]]
- Costs: 6 anamnecytes
- Permissions: Murder; Destruction of Machinery
- Health: 150

The Rogue is the Cortex defector.
It keeps some of the enforcement world's instincts, but turns them toward rot, betrayal, and flexible aggression.

## World Role
- Rogues are the most flexible Scum fate.
- They are a Controller path that flips into hostility.
- They sit between direct rebellion and institutional knowledge.
