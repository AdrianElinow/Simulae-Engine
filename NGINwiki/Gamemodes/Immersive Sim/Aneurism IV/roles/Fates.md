# Fates

Fates are who the player takes control of during possession. Before possession, you are the [[Spirit]].
Each fate sees the world differently.

## How They Work
- Every fate has an alignment, faction, role package, cost, permissions, and health pool.
- Most fates earn Anamnecytes by doing the work their role is built around.
- Several fates upgrade into higher or more extreme versions of themselves.
- The city reacts differently depending on whether you are Neutral, Cortex, or Scum.

## Alignment Groups
### Neutral
No direct affiliation to a faction.
- [[Spirit]]
- [[Prole]]
- [[Corpsman]]
- [[Liquidator]]
- [[Dealer]]
- [[Banker]]

### Cortex
Enforcers who keep the peace, execute Limitation Orders, confiscate Contraband, and enforce order and productivity.
- [[Controller]]
- [[Sanitar]]
- [[Limitator]]

### Scum
Anarchists, rebels, and cultists dedicated to tearing down Cortex oppression and propagating the Rot.
- [[Scumbag]]
- [[Zealot]]
- [[Rogue]]
- [[Malpractitioner]]

### Spoiler Fates
These are punishment or transformation states.
- [[Slave]]
- [[Vomit Coffin]]

## Relationship Map
- [[Prole]] is the entry point for most new players.
- [[Dealer]] leads into [[Banker]] and connects the player to the Night Market.
- [[Corpsman]] keeps bodies healthy and can become a [[Malpractitioner]].
- [[Liquidator]] cleans the city and can be promoted into [[Sanitar]].
- [[Controller]] polices the city and can become a [[Limitator]] or defect into [[Rogue]].
- [[Scumbag]] becomes [[Zealot]] when the Rot becomes faith.
- [[Slave]] is the penalty state for negative Anamnecytes, and [[Vomit Coffin]] is the classified end state beyond it.

## World Relationship
The game's factions are built around a simple conflict:
- Cortex wants order, productivity, and controlled access.
- Scum wants Rot, sabotage, and collapse.
- Neutral fates keep the city functional long enough for either side to matter.
