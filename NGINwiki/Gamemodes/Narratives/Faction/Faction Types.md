# Faction Types

Faction narratives should understand what kind of institution they are dealing with before they build quests, promotions, leadership changes, or conflicts. A mercenary company should not behave like a mage guild, and a criminal cartel should not use the same promotion logic as a knightly order.

## Core Archetypes

| Type | Narrative Focus | Typical Player Interactions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mercenary / PMC | Contracts, loyalty for pay, and professional reputation. | Join for money, pick employers, complete contracts, take better assignments, or lead a squad. |
| Corporation | Profit, hierarchy, promotion, and internal politics. | Get hired, climb the ladder, sabotage rivals, expose wrongdoing, or seize a division. |
| Criminal Organization | Secrecy, turf, smuggling, intimidation, and betrayal. | Prove loyalty, carry out crimes, control territory, frame enemies, or take over the crew. |
| Military | Chain of command, campaigns, logistics, discipline, and honor. | Enlist, earn rank, follow orders, disobey, desert, win medals, or lead a unit. |
| Spy Agency | Secrets, assets, dead drops, cover identities, and leverage. | Accept covert jobs, recruit assets, survive compromise, or become the handler. |
| Guild | Skill mastery, credentials, commissions, and craft politics. | Apprentice, earn certification, complete commissions, guard trade secrets, or become a guildmaster. |
| Knightly Order | Oaths, service, honor, relics, and succession. | Take vows, prove worth, undertake holy or chivalric quests, and rise through the order. |
| Mages Guild | Research, magical practice, arcane politics, and forbidden knowledge. | Study, cast, duel, publish, steal grimoires, and compete for leadership. |
| Religious Order / Cult | Faith, doctrine, ritual, conversion, and schism. | Convert, pilgrim, proselytize, manage rites, or split from orthodoxy. |
| Civic Movement / Political Bloc | Legitimacy, reform, elections, and public support. | Organize, campaign, legislate, protest, negotiate, or lead a reform movement. |
| Trade Consortium / Merchant House | Supply, routes, tariffs, monopoly, and market control. | Haul cargo, secure trade routes, undercut rivals, and acquire markets. |
| Academic / Research Group | Knowledge, grants, rival theories, and discoveries. | Publish, attend lectures, steal research, sponsor expeditions, and head a department. |

## Hybrid Factions
- Mercenary corporation: paid violence with branding, contracts, and a professional chain of command.
- Criminal state: organized crime that controls government offices, police, or customs.
- Military junta: armed leadership that claims the state through force.
- Intelligence corporation: data brokering, surveillance, and privatized secrets.
- Theocracy: a political faction whose legitimacy comes from religion.
- Academic cult: research, doctrine, and social pressure blended into one institution.

## Shared Faction Systems
- Rank ladders with clear gates, titles, and privileges.
- Reputation and trust that affect access to missions and leadership.
- Internal factions, rival candidates, and power blocs.
- Resource pools such as money, recruits, territory, influence, knowledge, or relics.
- Explicit enemies, allies, clients, and dependent groups.
- Signature rituals such as oaths, exams, initiation rites, induction ceremonies, and councils.

## Plot Lines By Faction Type
- Mercenary / PMC: contract runs, escort jobs, extraction missions, bounty hunts, mutiny, employer betrayal, and veteran promotion.
- Corporation: onboarding, audits, product security, sabotage, mergers, hostile takeovers, whistleblowing, and executive succession.
- Criminal Organization: initiation, rackets, smuggling, turf wars, debt collection, informant hunting, and boss succession.
- Military: enlistment, drills, deployment, siege response, morale crises, desertion, martial law, and command promotion.
- Spy Agency: recruitment, dead drops, surveillance, mole hunts, double agents, extraction operations, and handler promotion.
- Guild: apprenticeship, exams, commissions, accreditation, monopoly defense, master elections, and hidden craft disputes.
- Knightly Order: vows, trials of arms, relic recovery, honor duels, succession, heresy purges, and oath-breaking.
- Mages Guild: scholarship, spell licensing, forbidden archives, magical accidents, duels, and archmage politics.
- Religious Order or Cult: conversion, pilgrimages, ritual crises, schisms, holy wars, and doctrinal enforcement.
- Political Bloc: campaigns, legislation, endorsements, scandal management, coups, coalitions, and legitimacy struggles.

## Narrative Differences By Type
- Mercenary factions should mostly generate contracts, pay disputes, rescue jobs, and employer swaps.
- Corporate factions should mostly generate promotions, audits, sabotage, acquisitions, and board politics.
- Criminal factions should mostly generate rackets, smuggling, extortion, heists, and retaliation.
- Military factions should mostly generate deployments, drills, campaigns, logistics, and discipline stories.
- Spy agencies should mostly generate covert ops, dead drops, double agents, and identity games.
- Guilds and knightly orders should mostly generate tests, rank trials, service quests, and symbolic rewards.
- Mage guilds should mostly generate research, spell access, duels, apprenticeships, and forbidden lore.
