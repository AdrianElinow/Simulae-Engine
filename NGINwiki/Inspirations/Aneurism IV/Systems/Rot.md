# Rot

Rot is the city's corruption pressure.
It grows as bodies, systems, and infrastructure are left to decay.

## How It Works
- Rot makes the world nastier and the economy harder to run.
- Higher Rot increases friction for jobs, supplies, and city services.
- Rot is one of the main reasons the city feels like it is rotting from the inside out.

## Thresholds
- Enough Rot can trigger the Limitation Order.
- Critical Rot can trigger the Rotting Event.
- Scum and Zealots tend to treat Rot as fuel instead of a problem.

## World Role
- Rot is both a threat and a resource.
- Cortex wants to burn it out.
- Scum wants to spread it.
