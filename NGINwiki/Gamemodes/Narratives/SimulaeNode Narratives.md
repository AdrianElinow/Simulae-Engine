# SimulaeNode Narratives

This page maps narrative generation onto the actual [[SimulaeNode]] types. The generator should treat each node type as a different story surface with its own arc shapes, rewards, failure modes, and player interactions.

Use [[Plot Catalogue]] as the cross-cutting list of named plot types.

## Explicit Plot Types By Node
- POI plots include origin stories, apprenticeship, rivalry, hidden identity, exile, inheritance, corruption, redemption, missing person arcs, and succession.
- PTY plots include crew formation, recruitment, loyalty tests, mutiny, roster replacement, leadership disputes, shared missions, and disbanding.
- LOC plots include founding, ownership disputes, occupation, siege, renovation, infestation, quarantine, hidden passages, and abandonment.
- OBJ plots include theft, recovery, gifting, contraband, relic hunts, crafting, forgery, destruction, and curse or cleansing arcs.
- FAC plots include recruitment, initiation, rank climbs, internal audits, schisms, alliance building, war, and leadership contests.
- EVT plots include triggers, revelations, public ceremonies, covert meetings, escalations, outcomes, and aftermath chains.
- CND plots include access gates, reputation gates, time gates, secrecy gates, evidence gates, and relationship thresholds.

## POI - Person Narratives
- Origin stories: birth, upbringing, training, trauma, exile, and recruitment.
- Career arcs: apprenticeship, promotion, specialization, retirement, disgrace, and reinvention.
- Identity arcs: secret identities, double lives, hidden lineage, lost memories, and chosen names.
- Relationship arcs: friendship, rivalry, mentorship, loyalty, jealousy, romance, and betrayal.
- Power arcs: gaining, using, and losing access to skills, status, titles, and authority.
- Crisis arcs: injury, corruption, blackmail, illness, imprisonment, assassination, and redemption.

## PTY - Party Narratives
- Formation arcs: assembling a crew, fellowship, squad, retinue, or strike team.
- Roster arcs: new members, departures, replacements, defections, and rival claimants.
- Command arcs: leadership disputes, chain-of-command changes, and delegated authority.
- Cohesion arcs: morale, trust, internal friction, favorite allies, and group rituals.
- Operational arcs: shared missions, convoy runs, heists, investigations, and extraction jobs.

## LOC - Location Narratives
- Founding arcs: settlement building, outpost creation, district formation, and base establishment.
- Ownership arcs: claims, inheritance, occupation, eviction, eviction resistance, and administrative control.
- Danger arcs: siege, infestation, haunting, contamination, quarantine, blockade, and demolition.
- Discovery arcs: hidden rooms, secret routes, buried ruins, lost chambers, and forbidden wings.
- Growth arcs: renovation, expansion, specialization, prestige, decay, and abandonment.

## OBJ - Object Narratives
- Acquisition arcs: theft, purchase, salvage, inheritance, recovery, and gift exchanges.
- Artifact arcs: relic hunts, cursed items, prototype tools, legendary weapons, and unique quest items.
- Ownership arcs: contested property, evidence, contraband, confiscation, and restitution.
- Crafting arcs: blueprints, materials, refinement, upgrades, and signature equipment.
- Destruction arcs: breaking, losing, sabotaging, hiding, forging, or recontextualizing an object.

## FAC - Faction Narratives
- Recruitment arcs: being noticed, tested, accepted, and initiated.
- Reputation arcs: trust, standing, titles, favoritism, suspicion, and public legitimacy.
- Mission arcs: contracts, directives, orders, favors, errands, and strategic assignments.
- Power arcs: promotions, coups, leadership contests, schisms, reforms, mergers, and dissolution.
- Conflict arcs: alliances, betrayals, raids, embargoes, retaliations, and wars.

## EVT - Event Narratives
- Trigger arcs: events that begin a story, reveal information, or force a response.
- Outcome arcs: events that permanently alter memory, reputation, territory, or ownership.
- Chain arcs: one event that spawns another event, contract, rumor, or crisis.
- Public arcs: festivals, trials, elections, executions, ceremonies, and proclamations.
- Hidden arcs: secret meetings, dead drops, poisonings, blackmail exchanges, and covert actions.

## CND - Condition Narratives
- Access gates: rank checks, key possession, location presence, or relationship thresholds.
- Escalation gates: crime levels, faction hostility, public attention, or time pressure.
- Branch gates: whether a character has a secret, a promise, a witness, or a debt.
- Trigger gates: conditions that unlock a faction promotion, romance scene, assassination window, or story beat.

## Cross-Type Story Combinations
- POI + FAC: a person rises inside a faction, challenges authority, or defects with consequences.
- LOC + FAC: a faction claims territory, loses a base, or turns a district into a stronghold.
- OBJ + FAC: a faction wants a relic, a shipment, a secret file, or a symbolic crown.
- POI + OBJ: a person protects, steals, or inherits an important object.
- PTY + FAC: a party becomes the faction's strike team, retinue, or independent rival crew.
- EVT + CND: an event opens or closes a branch in a story chain.

## Generation Notes
- Each node type should have default story templates and randomizers.
- Narrative choices should read back from memory, relations, policy, and existing obligations.
- The generator should be able to escalate a single node change into a larger campaign arc when the surrounding state supports it.
