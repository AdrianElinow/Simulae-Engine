# Spirit

The Spirit is the pre-possession state whose only purpose is to become a living being.

- Alignment: Neutral
- Roles: Become Alive
- Costs: N/A anamnecytes
- Permissions: Possess
- Health: N/A

The Spirit is who you are before you choose a fate.

## How It Relates
- Every other fate starts from the Spirit.
- The Spirit is the bridge between spectator state and active play.
- Once you possess a fate, the city begins reacting to your alignment and role.
