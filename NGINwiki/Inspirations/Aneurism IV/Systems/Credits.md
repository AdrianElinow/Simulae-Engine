# Credits

Credits are the city's standard money.
They are used to buy goods, pay for services, own property, and participate in the normal economy.

## How They Work
- Earn Credits from work, sales, contracts, and some activities.
- Spend Credits on items, services, and market access.
- A paycycle distributes or refreshes part of the city's economy on a timer.
- A Banker can keep Credits between lives in ways other fates cannot.

## World Role
- Credits are the stable economy layer.
- They do not replace Anamnecytes; they sit beside them.
- Rot, scarcity, and events can make the Credit economy more or less valuable.
