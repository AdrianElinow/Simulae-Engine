# Implements

Implements are the tools used to perform tasks and interact with the world.
They are the hands of the city's labor economy.

## How They Work
- Implements are often reusable.
- They are used for repair, utility, containment, labor, and specialized interactions.
- Many of them are not weapons, but they can still be used aggressively or creatively.

## Common Groups
- Instruments: labor, utility, containment, adjuncts, and unusual tools.
- Supplies: repair kits, cleaning gear, medical supplies, and other consumables tied to work.

## World Role
- Implements are what make a job possible.
- They are the cleanest expression of the game's "use the object, not the menu" design.
