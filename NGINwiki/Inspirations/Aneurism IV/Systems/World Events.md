# World Events

World events are server-wide changes that continue whether or not a player starts them.
They are the city's way of forcing the map to evolve.

## Major Events
- [[Ration Cycle]]: the city issues food support on a timer.
- War: Cortex and labor systems shift into wartime production and the city opens new combat opportunities.
- Darkness: a Zealot-driven ritual event that turns the city into a more hostile and occult place.
- Limitation Order: Cortex can enter the Underground without being limited for a period of time.
- Rotting Event: Rot reaches a critical threshold and the city begins collapsing under corruption pressure.

## What They Do
- Events raise or lower access.
- Events change prices, safety, and legal exposure.
- Events create opportunities for certain fates and dead ends for others.
- Ignoring an event usually makes the next one worse.
