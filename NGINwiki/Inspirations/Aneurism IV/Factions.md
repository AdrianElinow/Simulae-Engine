# Factions

ANEURISM IV is organized around a few major factional pressures.
They shape what a fate can do, who it can work for, and how the city reacts to it.

## Main Factions
- P.L.F. (People's Labor Force): the labor and maintenance world behind the Neutral work fates.
- M.B. (Midnight Bloc): the trade and money world behind the Dealer and Banker line.
- Cortex: the enforcement state behind Controller, Sanitar, and Limitator.
- Scum: the rot and rebellion faction behind Scumbag, Zealot, Rogue, and Malpractitioner.

## Why They Matter
- Factions control role identity and upgrade paths.
- They explain which systems a fate can use most naturally.
- They define the city's main conflict: order, labor, trade, and rot.
