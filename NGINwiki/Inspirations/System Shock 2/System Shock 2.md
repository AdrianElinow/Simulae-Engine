# System Shock 2

System Shock 2 is a survival-horror immersive sim about a compromised ship, scarce resources, and layered systems that keep opening up as the player grows.
It is a benchmark for narration through logs, class identity, and hostile infrastructure.

## Core Loop
1. Start from a class or role that biases the early build.
2. Explore a damaged ship, collect logs, and recover tools and supplies.
3. Spend limited upgrades on hacking, weapons, psionics, or support skills.
4. Push deeper while managing damage, inventory, ammo, and the ship's defenses.

## Required Systems
- Class or career starts that meaningfully alter early capabilities.
- Hacking, repairs, inventory management, and terminal interaction.
- Psionic or energy-based abilities with their own resource economy.
- Audio logs and environmental storytelling as primary narrative delivery.
- Survival pressure from scarce supplies and hostile systems.

## Factions
- TriOptimum: the corporate layer behind the Von Braun disaster.
- The Unified National Nominate: the military and political frame around the player's career choice.
- SHODAN: the returning AI antagonist and manipulator.
- The Many: the psychic hive-mind infection that drives the horror.
- XERXES and ship infrastructure: the hostile systems layer that becomes its own opposition.

## Signature NPCs
- The Soldier or Hacker: the player avatar as a vulnerable role-based build.
- Dr. Janice Polito: the early guide who turns out to be SHODAN's mask.
- SHODAN: the game's defining villain and information-age threat.
- Thomas "Tommy" Suarez and Rebecca Siddons: late-game survivor fixtures.
- Captain William Bedford Diego: the military counterpoint in the ship story.
- Rebecca Lansing, as the broader series touchstone, and the various crew survivors encountered through audio logs and apparitions.

## Signature Weapons and Items
- Wrench: the baseline survival tool.
- Pistol, shotgun, assault rifle, and alien weapons: the main combat ladder.
- Laser rapier: the iconic high-risk melee choice.
- Psi Amp: the core psionic channel.
- Cyber-modules, nanites, ICE-picks, maintenance tools, hypos, and research items: the resource and upgrade layer.
- Security devices, vending machines, and dead ship hardware: interactable system objects the player can bend.

## Psionic and Survival Layer
- Offensive bolts, shields, healing, and telekinetic-style powers through OSA-style psionics.
- Hacking and repair as build-defining specialties.
- Research, weapon degradation, and ammunition type matching as pressure systems.
- Respawn machines, inventory scarcity, and hostile ecology as the reason every mistake matters.

## Enemy Types
- Hybrids and other infected crew forms.
- Cyborg Midwives and Cyborg Assassins.
- Security robots and hostile system drones.
- The Many's broader biomass and SHODAN's manipulations as the map-scale danger.

## Narrative Shape
- Station-wide crisis told through fragments of recorded history and abandoned spaces.
- A mix of horror, mystery, and systemic exploration rather than a linear plot march.
- The setting itself should feel like a machine that is trying to kill the player.

## Simulae Notes
- Best reference for combining survival tension with flexible systemic problem solving.
- Good benchmark for letting story emerge from logs, spaces, and failures instead of constant cutscenes.
- Strong model for class-driven starts, harsh resource loops, and AI opposition that feels systemic rather than scripted.
